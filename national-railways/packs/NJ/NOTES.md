# New Jersey (NJ) pack notes

## Region merges (phase 1)

**Outcome:** the merges as applied are right: 21 counties, 21 regions, nothing merged (`STATES.NJ` has an empty `merge`). Every county is over 50,000 (smallest: Salem 65k, then Warren 110k and Cape May 95k), so the rule (counties under 50,000 join up to 2 small neighbours) has nothing to join, and no exception is needed.

Checked against `packs/NJ/raw/counties.json`:
- Neighbour lists are symmetric and match the real map (Essex touches Bergen, Hudson, Morris, Passaic, Union; Burlington touches Atlantic, Camden, Mercer, Monmouth, Ocean; Hudson-Union is a contact across Newark Bay, minor). Outside links: Pennsylvania (Bucks, Philadelphia, Delaware County, Monroe, Pike, Northampton) and Delaware (New Castle, Sussex via the Cape May-Lewes Ferry bridge entry in `build-map.js`). No missing neighbour, no place in a wrong region that I found.
- Names are the automatic "X County, New Jersey": all read well and none clashes.
- Map oddity (not a merge problem): the simplified Monmouth shore cuts off the thin Sandy Hook spit (the Sandy Hook lighthouse at 40.46,-74.00 is water on the map), so the Sandy Hook town is placed on the map's land a little south (see Towns).
- The map's land includes Barnegat Bay and the other bays behind the barrier islands (every point I probed in Barnegat Bay, 39.70-39.80 N, is land of Ocean County), so nothing blocks track there and no `bays` are needed.

### Passes, bridges, water
- **Passes: none.** The map has no mountains in New Jersey (the Kittatinny Ridge and the Highlands are hills), and nothing the checker needs.
- **Bridges: none added** (as the brief said). The Delaware River crossings that New Jersey's lines use are all in `packs/PA/pack.js` (Benjamin Franklin, Walt Whitman, Betsy Ross, Tacony-Palmyra, Burlington-Bristol, Trenton-Morrisville, New Hope-Lambertville, Easton-Phillipsburg, the Water Gap, Milford-Montague, Matamoras-Port Jervis) and `packs/DE/pack.js` (the Delaware Memorial Bridge, the Cape May-Lewes Ferry, the Delaware Bay polygon). The map's only rivers here are the Delaware, Lehigh and Schuylkill; no lake or reservoir is in the terrain for the state, so there is no other water that needs a bridge. The Hudson, Newark Bay, Kill van Kull and Raritan Bay crossings are left for New York.
- **Bays: none.** The map's land includes Barnegat Bay, Great Bay and the bays behind the barrier islands, so track can cross them freely (the Manahawkin, Atlantic City Expressway and Ocean City causeways need no bridge points). The Delaware Bay polygon is in the Delaware pack.

- Not listed (farmland): the rest. No `dry`.
