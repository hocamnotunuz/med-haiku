## Pack (phase 2)

Files: `packs/VT/pack.js` (7 stations, 17 towns, 7 passes, 16 bridges, 3 levels, biomes) and this file. Populations and coordinates are from `packs/VT/raw/` (places.json points, 2 decimals; a station's `pop` is its region's population in thousands from counties.json). Anything hand-placed is named below. Bridge and pass points are from general knowledge and are good to about a mile.

### Start
`start: ["vt-chittenden", "vt-franklin-grand-isle", "vt-lamoille-orange-washington"]`: Burlington, St. Albans and Montpelier. Chittenden touches Franklin and Central, and Franklin touches Central (counties.json), so all three touch each other. Burlington is metro, St. Albans farm and Montpelier mountain: three types of place. The first track the pack test lays is Burlington to St. Albans (24 mi by the line, I-89 and US-7; 27 mi on the map) and 4 passengers arrive in the week. I took Franklin and Central rather than Addison-Rutland (98k) because the three form one compact north-western block on I-89 and the Levels start then has a clean first area; Rutland and Bennington make the second level.

### Passes (7; general knowledge; each has mountains within 7 mi in terrain.js, so the checker reports no warning)
The mountains in terrain.js are the Green Mountains' spine (about lon -72.95 to -72.8 from Rutland to Jay) and the southern Greens and Taconics near Manchester. Coordinates are the summits or the saddle, 2 decimals (sources: Wikipedia, USGS, VTrans route descriptions).
- **Bolton Gap** (I-89, the Winooski River and the old Central Vermont main line, Richmond to Waterbury, 44.37,-72.88): the Winooski cuts through the range here; this is the Burlington-Montpelier route. The brief offered "the Northfield Mountains (I-89)": the Northfield Range lies south of Montpelier and the I-89 gap that matters on the Burlington-Montpelier line is Bolton's; no line between my places crosses the Northfield Range, so it is not listed.
- **Smugglers' Notch** (VT-108, Stowe to Jeffersonville, 44.55,-72.79), **Appalachian Gap** (VT-17, Waitsfield to Bristol, 44.17,-72.92), **Middlebury Gap** (VT-125, Ripton to Hancock, 43.94,-72.96), **Brandon Gap** (VT-73, Brandon to Rochester, 43.85,-72.95), **Sherburne Pass** (US-4, Killington, 43.65,-72.82), **Hogback Mountain** (VT-9, Marlboro, the scenic overlook on the Bennington-Brattleboro road, 42.86,-72.92).
- Used by the game: Bolton, Smugglers', Appalachian, Middlebury, Brandon and Sherburne all come up on lines between the pack's places in both modes. **Hogback Mountain is on no line between my places**: the straight line from Bennington to Brattleboro crosses mountains thinner than the game's 4 mi, so it counts as hills (the checker accepts the pass at its real point; it is real and stays, and a player's line from further south or north may use it).
- Not added: Lincoln Gap (VT-17A, closed in winter, not a main route) and any Rutland-Manchester pass (US-7 and the railway climb Danby Mountain's saddle; I found no well-known named pass). In Tough, Rutland-Manchester needs a 7 mi tunnel (allowed).

### Bridges and ferries (16; general knowledge, sources the owners' pages and Wikipedia)
- **Lake Champlain** (the lake is water on the map and blocks track): Sand Bar Bridge (US-2, Milton to South Hero), the Swanton-Alburgh Bridge (VT-78, the Missisquoi Bay Bridge; point good to about a mile), the Rouses Point Bridge (US-2, Alburgh to Rouses Point, NY), the Lake Champlain Bridge (Chimney Point to Crown Point), and the Lake Champlain Transportation ferries Grand Isle to Plattsburgh (Gordon Landing), Charlotte to Essex and Burlington to Port Kent (a point mid-lake on the crossing). New York's pack lists none of these (its notes say they waited for Vermont), so they are added here.
- **Connecticut River** (the New Hampshire border; New Hampshire comes next): the Brooks Memorial Bridge (VT-9, Brattleboro to Chesterfield), the Vilas Bridge (Bellows Falls to Walpole), the Cornish-Windsor Covered Bridge (Windsor to Cornish, the longest covered bridge in the US), the White River Junction crossings (I-89, I-91 and the rail bridge to West Lebanon) and the Ledyard Bridge (Norwich to Hanover).
- **Winooski River** (on the map): Winooski Main Street Bridge (Burlington-Winooski), Richmond (I-89 and US-2), Waterbury (US-2 and the rail line) and Montpelier (US-2 and the State Street bridges), the crossings the Burlington-Montpelier line would use.
- Not on the map or not needed: US-4 Fair Haven-Whitehall and VT-9 Bennington-Troy cross land or small rivers the map does not draw. The checker gives no bridge warning (none is more than 3 mi from water).
